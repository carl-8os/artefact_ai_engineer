# Example 3 — Online return policy (illustrative)

**Customer:** I changed my mind about an online order. Can I return it?

**Assistant (illustrative):** For online purchases, the policy describes a 7-calendar-day withdrawal period after receipt. The store pays return shipping and states that refunds are issued to the original payment method within 10 business days. Store conditions must not override statutory consumer rights. Please contact the store team to arrange the return.

**Evidence:** Store policy, section 4.1. This is not a live LLM transcript.
